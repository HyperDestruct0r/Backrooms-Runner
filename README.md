# Backrooms Runner

**Backrooms Runner** is a browser-based, first-person exploration and survival game inspired by the Backrooms. Explore strange, procedurally generated environments, manage your resources, encounter entities, and search for a way forward.

**[Play Backrooms Runner](https://hyperdestruct0r.github.io/Backrooms-Runner/)**

## Features

- First-person 3D exploration powered by Three.js
- Multiple distinct, procedurally generated levels
- Seed-based worlds that expand as you explore
- Hostile and passive entities with different behaviors
- Flashlight, sprinting, crouching, and jumping
- Inventory, consumable items, and environmental activities
- Dynamic lighting, atmospheric effects, and immersive audio
- Player accounts, progression, and community chat
- Installable as a Progressive Web App

## The World

Each level offers a different environment, atmosphere, and set of challenges. From familiar yellow corridors and industrial tunnels to caves, abandoned neighborhoods, and open fields, the world is filled with unfamiliar places to discover.

Entities may be encountered throughout your exploration. Some are best avoided, while others may react to your actions.

Not everything is immediately explained. Exploration is part of the experience.

## Technology

Built with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and [Three.js](https://threejs.org/).
